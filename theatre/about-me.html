About Me

Jocelyne Lioe (she/her) is a Southeast Asian American artist originally from Tampa, Florida. She is currently based in Providence, Rhode Island and is pursuing a BA in Modern Culture and Media and a ScB in Computer Science at Brown University.

Theatre has been her light for some incredibly important times in her life and her love for the arts has only grown stronger. Singing and playing a variety of instruments since she was 4 years old, music already had its place in her heart. She found her love for acting and dancing in high school when she auditioned on a whim and fell into the loving arms of her theatre community.

Now, Jocelyne spends most of her time learning about as many facets of theatre as she can while polishing her current skills and strengths. While she is primarily a singer/actor and a stage manager, she also has a lot of experience in a variety of production and crew roles.

In her free time, Jocelyne likes to illustrate, harmonize, and code fun personal projects.

Please reach out anytime! Happy to talk!
